Coverage & reading notes

Killigan’s Treasure is a furry visual novel by Eddio. This unofficial guide to Public v0.57a covers journey choices, relationships and Personality differences, and the conditions for Memories, Codex entries, and equipment.

Coverage runs from the opening through the end of Blueleaf Grove Day 12, after the evening camp scene. Day numbers follow the dates shown in that part of the game.

The guide contains story and branch spoilers. Read the detailed steps after reaching the relevant scene. On a first playthrough, start with your current region; returning players and anyone with save or unlock questions can begin with Help.
