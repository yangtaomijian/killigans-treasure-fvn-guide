# Dressing Room: outfits, expressions and unlocks

For Killigan’s Treasure Public v0.57a. **Dressing Room** brings together selectable expressions and clothing appearances, arranged as **Character → Expressions / Outfit**. Item acquisition, stats and wearing restrictions are covered in [Equipment](equipment.md); menu and migration help is in [Help](../help.md#help-item-status).

## Dressing Room locator {#dressing-room-locator}

Match **Character → Outfit category → the value in the circular control** in your game, then select a missing value to see how it opens. The numbers match the game, including `3_r` / `3_h`; gaps do not imply missing options. **The number of options is not a count of different garments.** `3_r` opens when you receive the special competition garment, Prime Attire; `3_h` opens after the competition. In this release, selecting either option does not display its garment, so do not treat them as two separate clothing appearances to collect.

Category names below help identify the game’s part icons. **A category with only one option normally has no switching icon.** These are the targets reachable through ordinary play in this release; region availability and adult-content settings also affect your menu. See [Expressions below](#dressing-room-expressions) for how viewing history opens expressions.

## Dressing Room structure {#choose-a-character-then-adjust-expressions-and-outfit-parts}

The entrance is **Gallery → Dressing Room**. The side arrows switch region or group, and a character entry opens that character's Expressions and Outfit. Starting groups include **Treasure Crew, Redroot, Redroot Wilds, Aris, Arcane Academy, Athlima Summit, Aris (contd.)**. **Crystal Plains** and **???** join later in the journey, and Zhokhar joins Treasure Crew. Expressions and outfit options expand as their individual unlock conditions are met.

- **Expressions is on the left:** expand the list to choose an expression, independently of outfit parts.
- **Outfit is on the right:** part icons switch category; the up and down arrows cycle that part's available options. Unlocked parts can be freely combined.
- **Parts vary by character:** Killigan can change upper-body clothing, belt, footwear, map display, bottoms, underwear, and weapons once there are alternatives. Some characters also have accessories, colour or character-specific appearance changes. Adult presentation also depends on content settings. These descriptions identify the parts represented by icons; a switching icon appears when that part has multiple available appearances.

## Expressions {#expressions-viewed-appearances-are-not-a-completion-total}

**Selectable expressions grow as those expressions appear during regular story play.** The list generally follows viewing history on this device, so counts vary by character and progress; some characters have a starting expression. Killigan's expressions also unlock through his story appearances. Expressions are independent of equipment ownership, partner confirmation and outfit collection.

To revisit an expression, load a regular save from before its scene, see the character's appearance, then return to Dressing Room. This reference follows selectable expressions and appearances; outfit combinations and image variations are not counted as separate collectibles.

## Outfit: default, automatic and optional unlocks {#outfit-default-automatic-and-optional-unlocks}

Default appearances come with a character; story appearances join at their journey points; optional garments unlock through rewards or purchases. **Acquired appearance unlocks persist across saves, while physical items in a story save follow that save's acquisition record.** Missed rewards and choices can be revisited from regular saves before the relevant windows.

## Equipment, wearing and appearance unlocks {#check-ownership-wearing-and-appearance-unlocks-separately}

::: {.kt-adaptive-records}

| State | Record |
|---|---|
| Equipment ownership | Items in the current story save, shown in **Equipment**; later this also includes the shared trunk. |
| Wearing | The equipped state in **Equipment**, which determines current stats or combat use. |
| Dressing Room appearance unlock | Persistent character, part and appearance options on this device, independent of the current story save's equipped state. |
| Post-Summit memento | The memento record in **Supplies**, for unwearable items. |

:::

The three Blueleaf Grove evening gifts use [Equipment's three-consecutive-night steps](equipment.md#blueleaf-gifts). Equipment acquisition and new selectable appearances do not have a one-to-one correspondence. Backpack Map ownership / wearing and region map lists are also distinct records. See the [Help state table](../help.md#help-state-table).

## Recovery and older saves {#check-a-missing-option-in-this-order}

Expressions can be revisited from regular saves before their scenes; optional garments from before reward, purchase or branch windows; automatic appearances at their story points. Trailmarkers return to story positions, while viewing and outfit records follow actual appearances and acquisitions.

Lists after loading older saves or changing devices may differ with the viewing and appearance records retained. Save and migration details are in [returning-player Help](../help.md#help-trailmarkers).
