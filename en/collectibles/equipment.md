# Equipment & Supplies

<span id="equipment-outfits-and-useful-items"></span>

For Killigan’s Treasure Public v0.57a. Follow the journey through **all 35 optional wearable items**, including ordinary shop stock, then **11 automatic story items**. Simple purchases take one row; conditional rewards have detailed steps.

This covers normal acquisition opportunities. Branch gifts can be mutually exclusive, and early garments may be removed by the story. Supplies, mementos, regional map lists and appearance-only unlocks are separate.

For appearance values and Expressions, use [Dressing Room](dressing-room.md). If an obtained item seems missing, check [Help](../help.md#help-item-status) and [equipment care](#equipment-state-care).

<a id="silvatto-garments"></a>

### Silvatto's three garments

After cutting wood in Redroot, deliver the armor order to Silvatto to reach his reward screen. To obtain **Silvatto Vest**, **Silvatto Loinwrap**, and **Silvatto Anklets**, select each one and answer `Yep.` at `Get it?`. The screen returns after each item, so keep choosing until you have all three. `Nah.` only returns without taking that garment. Finish the conversation once the set is complete.

The physical garments are removed during the later escape. Their **Dressing Room** appearances can remain in the outfit list across saves. To recover a missed appearance, load a regular save from before the reward screen. See the [Redroot journey](../guide/redroot.md#redroot).

### Marchanide Langou: getting and wearing it

On Redroot Wilds Day 8, Cabotte gives Killigan a Marchanide Langou and equips it automatically **if you lose the fight, or if the Belt is no longer worn at the post-win check**. You see `Obtained Marchanide Langou`. Win while still wearing the Belt instead and you do not get the langou at this point, but can receive Cabotte's invitation that evening. Wearing the Belt before the fight is not enough if it breaks and comes off during combat. Re-equip a repaired Belt before going out. Save before the Day 8 cry for help, while you can still change equipment, to compare the branches. See the [scene and two Memories](../guide/redroot.md#redroot-wilds-day8).

Check the physical langou under **Equipment → Undies** in the current save. A **Dressing Room** appearance unlock is a separate, persistent state.

If you miss the early branch, the story gives you Marchanide Langou when leaving Spiceport for Blueleaf Grove if you still do not own it. This later acquisition does not restore the early branch or change the Aris gift you already received.

You can choose only one on this visit; save before the question to keep both acquisition outcomes. The gift choice does not appear if you already have Marchanide Langou. The shop's **Aris Langou** is different from Macsen's Spare Langou; do not identify them by appearance or category alone. Borrowing a langou to model for Macsen's report on Day 4 is part of that scene, not a new physical equipment award. See the [early Aris journey](../guide/aris.md).

If you chose Spare, unequip and sell it via `Sell`, then re-enter the shop on a later listed shop day to buy Aris Langou; storing Spare in the trunk does not restore the stock. Keep a pre-question save for the alternate gift outcome. Choosing Spare does not prevent buying Hand-me-down Loincloth later.

### Days 5–8: Prime Attire {#prime-attire}

**Prime Attire is the special competition garment awarded after completing the preparations below.** The ordinary competition garment is called Summit Attire.

**Where and when:** The Coliseum in Aris. Complete the preparation, then claim Prime on your **first Coliseum entry of Day 7 or Day 8**. Each entry costs two time slots. Day 8 is a recovery chance for the reward, not a new chance at the earlier rumor or armor help.

Each Coliseum entry costs two slots and allows at most two spars. Covering Matagoffe, Tofi and Atama takes at least two entries. **Finish each spar; you need not win or use a fixed order.** After the first, rematch or change opponents; leave after the second.

#### Already partway there? Check four things

1. **Old attire rumor:** Hear `Listen in on a conversation about summit attire` once at Skrilzi Commons on Days 5–7. This option is unavailable on Day 8.
2. **Armor help:** Choose `Help out the hardscale trio` at Skrilzi Commons on Day 6 or 7 and finish the event with Tofi and the others. On Day 6, you can hear the rumor and help on the same visit. The Wits icon beside this choice does not add a separate stat gate. Finishing the help also supplies the needed skrilzi support.
3. **Local support:** On Day 5, choose `Help someone move crates` at Bestial Alleys, or `Donate to a beggar` if you have at least **5 Ouros**. On Day 7, choose `Inspire fellow beasts`, or `Donate to a beggar` if you have at least **5 Ouros**; `Help someone clean up a wrecked stall` does not provide the support Prime needs. Before your first Day 8 Coliseum entry, that day's Bestial Alleys or Skrilzi Commons activity can also supply it. These are alternatives; you need not do them all.
4. **Three opponents:** Complete a Coliseum spar with each of Matagoffe, Tofi, and Atama. If one is still missing, pick that opponent after your first bout. Once all three are covered, the remaining second bout may be a repeat.

The ordinary **Summit Attire** arrives earlier in the story; it does not replace these four preparations.

#### Missed Day 7: can you claim it on Day 8?

Yes, if **everything is ready before your first Day 8 Coliseum entry** and you have two slots left. If your first Day 7 entry still lacks the last opponent, you can finish that spar then and claim Prime on your first Day 8 entry. If only local support is missing on Day 8, first do the day's Bestial Alleys or Skrilzi Commons activity. The Commons activity that day cannot recover the older rumor or armor help.

Finishing a condition after your first Day 8 entry will not trigger the reward on a second visit. If the rumor or armor help is gone, or both claim days have passed, return to an earlier regular save where you can complete what is missing. Day 7 Shipyard leads into the evening event; resting at the Tavern ends free exploration. Plan the Coliseum before either one.

### Blueleaf Grove: gifts from three consecutive talks

**Save before your first `Talk` on Day 10 evening.** On Day 9, you can speak to all three characters; that night does not count for gifts. On each of Days 10, 11, and 12, you have time for one full evening talk with **Macsen, Cabotte, or Zhokhar**. Opening a talk and choosing `Nevermind` does not count. Miss a night or switch people, and this release has no fourth night to recover it. One continuous playthrough can get at most one gift. Neither a formal relationship nor a Day 11 spar win is required. See the [daily plan](../guide/blueleaf-grove.md#blueleaf-day10).

## Equipment state and care {#equipment-state-care}

1. In **Equipment**, check the item name and category in this save, then whether it is worn. Only worn gear provides wearing bonuses.
2. Later, also check the shared trunk if the category slot is full. Appearance unlocks and Supplies mementos are not wearable gear. See [state differences](../help.md#help-state-table).
3. Repair damage at a camp that offers repairs. Free and hammer opportunities are explained under [Repair Hammer](#practical-items). **Check worn state again after repair**, especially before comparing a Belt-dependent fight.

4. Shops require free space in the relevant Equipment category. Move gear to the trunk where available, or use `Sell` to free space. Story gifts can fall back to the trunk when a category is full; shop purchases have a separate capacity check.

## Ouros & Shopping

**Reserve money for travel supplies and target items before wagering.** Check the income opportunities and shopping windows for your current location below.

### Aris: training, competition preparation and the banquet

- **Day 3:** Elise gives Ouros after the spar, with the amount depending on the result. Complete the story encounter and check what you receive.
- **Days 5–8:** arena wagering is optional. A win earns the stake, a loss costs it, and a tie changes nothing; higher wagers also make the spar harder. Each entry costs two slots for at most two bouts. Plan around supplies and the [four Prime Attire prerequisites](#prime-attire). [Combat and retries](../reference/combat.md#combat-goal).
- **Day 11 banquet:** choose `Take it` for 2000 Ouros, then check your [Repair Hammers / Rations / Bedroll](#practical-items) for departure. [Aris journey](../guide/aris.md).

### Crystal Plains and Shieldfall Vale: travel encounters

Winning Echo fights on Crystal Plains Days 2, 4 and 7 or Shieldfall Vale Day 5 gives extra Ouros. Check whether gear needs repair afterwards and keep enough supplies for camp and the journey ahead. [Echo approaches](../reference/combat.md#combat-echo).

### Spiceport: plan activity and shopping time first

Helping at the Nursery pays, and Fishing rewards depend on the catch. Plan the one-slot solo activities on Days 3–4, the two-slot Day 5 afternoon and Day 6 free exploration with the [regional guide](../guide/spiceport.md#spiceport-early-excursions), leaving room for early events and shopping. A repeat fishing session costs two slots. After the Nursery reopens on Day 5, that visit can remain available on Day 6 if you have yet to complete it.

**Accepting a character invitation on Day 6 takes you into their scene; choosing `Nah` or having no invitation leaves two free-exploration slots.** Save before answering if you want to compare income activities or shopping. The old-fashioned stock’s shop and back-room window is Day 5 afternoon or Day 6 free exploration; see [old-fashioned clothes](#spiceport-old-fashioned). The ordinary Day 8 shop visit lets you buy more supplies.

Each shop’s `Sell` page lists saleable items and offered prices. Keep enough supplies for the next part of the journey, then choose what to sell.
