### Two Day 8 entries: Cabotte's positions

Save before the cry for help on Day 8 and check that the **Belt is equipped**. Cabotte's invitation that night also requires beating Bijouk and **still wearing the Belt at the post-fight check**. It comes off automatically if broken in combat; a repaired Belt must have been re-equipped while gear changes were possible. Losing, or winning after the Belt has come off, continues the story without the invitation. Both outcomes give Marchanide Langou. At the inn, either drinking or eating continues its own version of the scene. Choose `Sure` if the invitation appears.

**From acceptance to the final choice:** If Killigan is sober and **NSFW Images** is `On`, `Sure` starts a clickable interaction before the position menu. Continue until `Move on?`, then choose `Yes, please.` to advance. `Nah, not yet.` delays it; you can still follow later prompts to continue. The drunk branch has its own outhouse question and transition before the final position choice, so do not look for the sober branch's `Move on?` there. At `Off`, the sober branch skips the clickable interaction, while both Memories remain temporarily locked under the NSFW replay rule.

- For the **earlier entry**, choose `I'll top.` while sober or `I'll give.` while drunk.
- For the **later entry**, choose `I'll bottom.` while sober or `I'll receive.` while drunk.

`You choose.` picks a position at random. One run through the scene takes only one; reload a regular save and choose the other to collect both deliberately. Both are marked NSFW. If **NSFW Images** is `Off`, they remain locked for replay even after their scenes have appeared; check again at `On`. Unlocking an entry does not mean every sober, drunk, or replay variation has been seen. See [Redroot Wilds Day 8](../guide/redroot.md#redroot-wilds-day8) for the equipment and relationship route.

### Macsen's balcony: Aris (p.2)

These are adjacent **Aris Day 11** entries in **Aris (p.2)**, both with a Macsen relationship icon. Use their scenes to tell them apart.

The image appears as Killigan begins confessing to Macsen on the balcony.

#### Later entry: balcony kiss

Macsen kisses Killigan afterward. These are **two separate Memories entries** that appear in order during one complete `Yes` branch. There is no choice between them or need for two routes.

#### How to get them

#### SFW and replay

Neither balcony entry is SFW-locked. Turning on SFW does not skip the corresponding story images. Each entry has replay variations; collecting it does not require viewing every variation in the story. This SFW note applies to these two entries only.

### Entries 4 and 5: two places to sleep

Ray's invitation requires that Killigan **did not formally confirm Macsen at the Aris balcony**. Use either of these activity plans. Save before the faith question, then check for the invitation at bedtime:

- Answer `Yes` to the faith question. Do `Bugs on a stick` and make them for `Ray`; then finish either dancing or ring toss. That last interaction can fail and the invitation can still appear.
- Answer `No` to the faith question. Complete **all three** night activities—dancing, ring toss, and bugs made for `Ray`—and succeed at dancing or ring toss at least once. Both parts of ring toss must succeed for that activity to count as a success. If both dancing and ring toss fail, this plan does not bring the invitation.

**Getting them that night:** You can continue to activities you have not done or end the night early. Ending too soon may prevent the invitation without stopping the main story. Both plans still depend on the Aris balcony relationship choice. Choose `Yes` when Ray invites you for **entry 4**. Choose `No`, or receive no invitation, and return to your own nest; there, choose `Sure` at the adult-scene question for **entry 5**.

**Compare or recover:** If Ray invites you, save before answering and reload for both `Yes` and `No`. Before ending the activities, you can do more if enough are left to meet a plan. Once the night is over without an invitation, return to an activity save; to change the faith answer, return to the earlier question. A save with Macsen confirmed at the Aris balcony will not get Ray's invitation: branch from before that decision. Ray's nest and Macsen's Shieldfall Vale scene cannot be collected in one continuous playthrough. See the [section route and save points](../guide/crystal-plains-shieldfall.md#question-mark-sequence).

Switch back to `On` and check again. The other six entries do not have that gallery lock. See [Help](../help.md#help-memories-lock) for settings questions.

### Two solo Bareleaf Park images and recovery

Although both entries have the single icon, you do not need to choose a single route first. A confirmed Macsen relationship or Zhokhar trial does not rule out the solo visit. Access depends on the schedule and previous park visits below.

**How to get them:** You can first visit the park alone for a walk on Day 3. A **second solo visit** on Day 4 offers `Rub one out`, `Pour one out`, and `Nothing`; the first two give entries 6 and 5 respectively. On Day 6, this is also possible if you reach the two-slot free-exploration map—either because no companion invites you or because you decline—and the solo park entrance is still available. That Day 6 entry uses the optional scene even if you did not finish the Day 3–4 park sequence.

**What Day 6 can recover:** A Day 3 solo first visit followed by a Day 4 solo return leaves no Day 6 solo park entry. A Day 3 solo visit followed by the Day 5 clutchmates outing also removes that Day 6 chance, whether or not you chose `Bond with them`. Check the Day 6 free map for the solo park entry first. Accepting a character's park invitation goes directly to their scene, without free exploration, and cannot replace either solo scene. See the [time plan](../guide/spiceport.md#spiceport-day6).

**How to compare them:** Save before the `Rub one out` / `Pour one out` choice and reload for the other answer. Do not plan on getting both in one continuous run by combining Day 4 and Day 6. Clothing and Personality change some lines or stat gains, not the gate for these two choices. Both entries are NSFW-locked; see [Help](../help.md#help-memories-lock) for replay settings.
