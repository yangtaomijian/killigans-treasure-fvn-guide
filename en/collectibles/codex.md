# Codex entries, updates, and extra pages

For **Killigan’s Treasure Public v0.57a**. Look up an English entry name under the Codex's five in-game tabs. Story spoilers follow. This page covers **56 entries**; new notes and extra pages belong to their existing entries. Codex progress is stored in a regular save, so notes from different branches do not merge. See the [journey overview](../index.md) or [Help with Codex and saves](../help.md#help-codex).

**Jump to:** [People (16)](#codex-people) · [Species (11)](#codex-species) · [Magic (13)](#codex-magic) · [History (8)](#codex-history) · [World (8)](#codex-world) · [Optional pages and recovery](#codex-special) · [Current-version limits](#codex-cutoff).

**Reading the tables:** “First appears” refers to the base Codex entry. “Later updates” gives the later time for a note, profile detail, or page. If a row says “No later update in this version,” there is no further update point to chase in Public v0.57a. The final column carries individual conditions, bookmark aliases, and lookup tips; its conditions apply to the entry or page named there. “—” means no extra note. “Starting” means present in a new regular playthrough, though the game does not prompt you to inspect the portable Codex until **Redroot Wilds Day 9**.

**What is an extra page?** It is a page within an existing Codex entry, such as **Cute Things He Does** under **People → Macsen**, not a new character bookmark. Macsen's base entry arrives at the time in the table; balcony `Yes` controls that extra page alone. Ordinary notes are labeled **Notes**. An extra page has its own English title and sometimes needs a particular choice. Page numbers may change with save progress, so look by **tab → entry → page title**.

## Optional pages: conditions and recovery

<a id="codex-macsen-cute-things"></a>

### Macsen · Cute Things He Does

Choose `Yes` at the [Aris Day 11 banquet balcony](../reference/relationships.md#macsen-balcony) to formally confirm Macsen. The normal Crystal Plains Day 10 Codex update then adds this page under **People → Macsen**. Day 8 `Romantic` or sharing a bed cannot replace the balcony decision. If you chose `No`, reload from **before that balcony question**; if you simply have not reached the Plains update, continue the story. A save already past the update is not guaranteed to fill in a missed extra page automatically.

<a id="codex-zhokhar-tease"></a>

### Zhokhar · Things to Tease About

Do **not** formally confirm Macsen at the Aris balcony. Enter Zhokhar's private talk after Spiceport Day 3 dinner and choose `Competition` to record the trial that you can later reconsider. The Spiceport Day 4 evening Codex update adds this page under **People → Zhokhar**. Formal relationship confirmation is on Day 7; you do not have to wait until then to look for this page. If you chose `Support`, reload no later than **before the Day 3 private-talk choice**. If the talk never appeared, you need an earlier companionship save and possibly a different Aris balcony branch. See [Relationships](../reference/relationships.md#zhokhar-spiceport). The Macsen and Zhokhar relationship pages cannot both be acquired in one continuous playthrough.

<a id="codex-balls-research"></a>

### Four characters · Balls Research (by Mac)

Choose `Join in` during the Blueleaf Grove Day 10 travel interaction. Before nightfall, that day's update adds a same-title page under **People → Killigan, Zhokhar, Cookie, and Cabotte**. These are still four existing character entries, not four new Codex entries. `Cool things down` skips the pages. The latest useful regular save is **before that Day 10 travel choice**. Talking with Macsen at camp or later filling in a missing base entry will not add these four pages for you. See the [Day 10 journey](../guide/blueleaf-grove.md#blueleaf-day10).

### Other changes worth checking

- **People → Cabotte:** Completing the optional intimate encounter on Redroot Wilds Day 8 adds an ordinary character note, not a separate page. You need the win and Belt still worn after combat for an invitation, then must accept and enter the scene. See [Relationships](../reference/relationships.md#cabotte-wilds). If there was no invitation, reload before the fight; if it appeared and you only want to compare acceptance or later choices, a save before the local choice is enough.
- <a id="codex-clutchmates"></a>**People → The Clutchmates:** First-visit training at the Coliseum during Aris Days 5–8 can add the base entry. If you train on **Day 5**, the bookmark is filled in at that evening's / Day 6 opening information update; continue there before assuming it is missing. Helping with armor on Day 6 or 7 can also fill in a missing base entry, then update the job field. If only that job field is missing, reload no later than **before Day 7 free exploration**. If the bookmark itself is absent, return to a save **before Day 8 free exploration**, while the Coliseum is still accessible. See the [Aris journey](../guide/aris.md).
- <a id="codex-aris-tax"></a>**World → Aris:** At **Bestial Alleys** on Day 6, choose `Sure` at `Check out the Bestial Alleys?` and finish the tax conversation. It uses **one slot** and adds an ordinary note to the existing Aris entry, not a new entry or extra page. If missed, return to before that day's free exploration. Day 6 has only three slots; the existing [Prime example](equipment.md#prime-attire) uses one at Commons and two at the Coliseum. Rearrange training if you also want the tax note. Prime still has its Day 8 recovery conditions.
- **Magic → Echoes / People → Banni / People → Killigan:** **Varieties** is added automatically on Crystal Plains Day 10. **Devotion to Va`Taur** and **Enraged** are added on Blueleaf Grove Day 10. Check Banni's Spiceport Day 8 base entry separately from his Grove Day 10 special page.

## Current-version limits

Spiceport has Codex updates on **Day 4 evening** and **Day 8**. Day 8 adds or checks Banni, Izashi, Seascale, and Banni's "Pirates", along with several character details. If those base entries are missing, Grove Day 10 offers another chance to fill them in.

Find pages by the in-game tab, entry name and extra-page title. For saves and chapter starts, see [Help](../help.md#help-trailmarkers).
