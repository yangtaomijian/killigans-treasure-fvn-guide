# Spiceport

For Killigan’s Treasure Public v0.57a. This volume runs from arrival through departure on Spiceport Day 8; Day numbers are those displayed in the city. Mild story hints follow.

::: {.kt-read-answer}
**Decide these two things first:**

- **Warehouse on Day 4 still completes the excursion, but is too late for its early use at Zhokhar's Day 3 talk.** [Days 2–4 plan](#spiceport-early-excursions).
- **You cannot make up Fishing after accepting a Day 6 character invitation.** `Sure` goes straight to the scene with **no free exploration**. `Nah` / no invitation gives **two slots**. [Day 6 branch](#spiceport-day6).

**Day 5's two-slot fishing and two-slot clutchmates outing cannot both fit. Save before that afternoon map, and again before answering the Day 6 invitation, to compare outcomes.**

## How to use this volume

Free exploration comes in two stretches. On **Days 2–4**, fit in the three two-slot companion excursions. On **Days 5–6**, divide time between the shop, fishing, and Bareleaf Park. Some activities have a later chance, depending on the day and your relationships. See [Spiceport Memories](../collectibles/memories.md#spiceport-memories) for same-title images, [Relationships](../reference/relationships.md#zhokhar-spiceport) for Zhokhar, and [Equipment](../collectibles/equipment.md#spiceport-old-fashioned) for the special outfits.

## Arrival and early choices

On Day 1, a confirmed Macsen relationship from Aris means you automatically share a room with him. Otherwise, you may sleep alone or share with Zhokhar. Sharing with Zhokhar changes one Day 2 morning Memory and brings you closer; it is not a formal relationship. Killigan's own Day 2 resting image appears with all three lodging arrangements. Save before choosing rooms if you want to compare the same-title images.

You also choose a meal plan. `Premium meal plan` is quoted at **1200 Ouros** and `Economy meal plan` at **400 Ouros**; the companions' contributions to the economy plan are handled in its payment conversation. Either plan allows the journey and images to continue. Meals display their stat gains: at the Day 4 dinner, for example, Premium shows **+6 each to Brawn, Charm, and Wits**, while Economy shows **+1 each**. The east-city map joins the map list during the early story.

## Days 2–4: companion excursions and return visits

**Decide first:** all three excursions can fit through Day 4; Warehouse must be earlier if you want its help for the Day 3 talk.

{{< include ../../_includes/spiceport-planning-en.md >}}

::: {.kt-adaptive-records .kt-travel-schedule}

| Day | Time available | A workable schedule |
|---|---:|---|
| Day 2 | Two slots | Market with Taavi, Boardwalk with Macsen, or Warehouse with Zhokhar. Each takes both slots, so choose one today. |
| Day 3 | Three slots | Spend one slot helping at Residential Streets or visiting Bareleaf Park alone for the first time, then take an unvisited companion excursion for two slots. |
| Day 4 | Three slots after meeting Cookie at Fishing Pier | Use one slot at Residential Streets if you have not been there, or Bareleaf Park (which can be a return visit), then take the last uncompleted two-slot excursion. |

:::

If you simply want **all three companion excursions**, take one on Day 2 and use one solo slot plus one excursion on each of Days 3 and 4. There is no single mandatory Day 2 choice. Complete the excursions by **Day 4** if they matter to you; do not rely on old map icons on Days 5–6 to make them up. Agreeing to look more closely at the Warehouse brings Zhokhar closer. Exploring with him can also change how his clothing is presented, but does not give Killigan equipment.



<details class="kt-read-more">
<summary>Nursery payment details</summary>

At the Residential Streets nursery, both `Help out` and `Leave` ultimately lead Killigan to help. The payment is **50 or 25 Ouros**. To aim for the higher amount, check Wits in Stats and wear gear that raises it before you receive the payment. Either result continues the story; you need not rebuild your whole stat plan for 25 Ouros.

</details>

 A first solo Bareleaf Park visit is a walk. A **second solo visit** brings up `Rub one out`, `Pour one out`, and `Nothing`; the first two give different Day 4-title Memories, and you choose only one per visit. A conditional solo return is possible on Day 6. See [recovery for those two images](../collectibles/memories.md#spiceport-bareleaf-solo).

For Zhokhar's private talk after the Day 3 dinner, the firm relationship prerequisite is **not having formally confirmed Macsen in Aris**. One workable preparation route is to choose `Help treat him` on Crystal Plains Day 5, share Zhokhar's room on Spiceport Day 1, and take the Warehouse excursion on Day 2 or 3 with `Sure` at `Get a closer look?`. These actions build closeness; **they are not all individually required**. If the private talk already appears after dinner, take it without replaying just to complete this example route. See [more preparation](../reference/relationships.md#prepare-zhokhar).

During the talk, `Competition` begins a trial you can reconsider and gives two Day 3 images. `Support` brings you closer without starting that trial. Formal confirmation waits until Day 7. See the [full relationship chain](../reference/relationships.md#zhokhar-spiceport). The Day 4 evening Codex update may add the corresponding extra page for your relationship state; the [Codex lookup](../collectibles/codex.md#codex-special) has its title and condition.

## Day 5: half a day free

{{< include ../../_includes/spiceport-recovery-en.md >}}

The morning library, crew, and harbor events follow the story. In the afternoon you have **two slots** and access to both east and west city maps.

::: {.kt-adaptive-records .kt-travel-schedule}

| Activity | Cost | Later chance? |
|---|---:|---|
| General Store | One slot | Can be revisited during Day 6 free exploration. You enter again on Day 8, but its special back-room topic is not on that menu. |
| Fishing Pier return | Two slots | Available during Day 6 free exploration. |
| Bareleaf Park with the three clutchmates | Two slots | This companion park activity is Day 5 only. A confirmed Macsen relationship removes its adult invitation. |
| Residential Streets nursery | One slot | Reopens on Day 5 even if you helped on Day 3 or 4. If you skip it today, it can carry into Day 6 free exploration. |
| Bridge | Ends the afternoon | You may go early, giving up unused time. |

:::

You cannot do the two-slot fishing trip and the two-slot clutchmates activity on the same Day 5. Fishing has a later opportunity **if Day 6 leads to free exploration**; the clutchmates outing does not. For their Day 5 Memory, reserve both slots for Bareleaf Park, have no confirmed Macsen relationship, and choose `Bond with them` once there. You need not have visited the park alone first. But if you went there alone on Day 3 and return with the clutchmates on Day 5, the solo park entry will be unavailable on the Day 6 free map, even if you did not choose `Bond with them`. Save before opening the afternoon map if you want to compare these two-slot plans.

For the old-fashioned shop collection, **successfully buy a wearable item** at General Store, then finish shopping. At `Any other business?`, choose the Charm-lock `Anything else in the back?` **when it is selectable**.

<details class="kt-read-more">
<summary>If the back-room topic is disabled or missing</summary>

If the back-room option is visible but unavailable, reload from before the purchase, adjust worn gear, and check current Charm in Stats. Buying only Rations or Repair Hammers does not open `Any other business?`; buy a wearable item successfully, then finish shopping.

</details>

 Choosing the topic adds five special items to that shop session; you still have to buy each one. This also works on Day 6 if you reach free exploration. Ordinary stock needs no Charm preparation. See [the items and wearing restrictions](../collectibles/equipment.md#spiceport-old-fashioned).

## Day 6: park invitation or free exploration

**Direct answer: after `Sure`, you cannot make up Fishing. It enters the character scene directly, with no free exploration. Choose `Nah`, or reach the no-invitation branch, for two slots. Save before answering.**

If Macsen is Killigan's confirmed partner, he invites Killigan to Bareleaf Park after the shipboard event. Without that relationship, Zhokhar invites him if they started the Day 3 trial. Accepting gives that character's Day 6 Memory. During free exploration, you can revisit Fishing Pier and General Store. The nursery carries over only if it reopened on Day 5 and you **did not do it then**; it does not refresh again on Day 6. Ship ends the remaining time early.

The solo Bareleaf Park option on Day 6 requires its map entry still to be open. It can show the same pair of solo scenes as Day 4, even if you did not complete the Day 3–4 park sequence. A Day 5 visit with the clutchmates can also affect this entry; see the [solo park conditions](../collectibles/memories.md#spiceport-bareleaf-solo).

## Day 7: beach and relationship decisions

The beach does not use map time slots. You can spend time with the clutchmates, Macsen, and Zhokhar in succession; your order does not remove the other two. When helping Macsen with his towel, choose the Wits-lock `Use special method` **only when selectable**. It brings you closer to him; the ordinary method still leads to sunbathing. Succeeding at the later Wits reaction gives a second Day 7 beach image, plus a small Wits gain. Failure still continues the beach outing. Save before the sunbathing interaction if you want that image.

With Zhokhar, the Brawn-lock `Let's race` must also be **selectable** to start the race. Its interaction changes the winner, nearby description, and stat growth, but is not a gate on the formal Day 7 relationship decision. A visible lock choice may still be unavailable. Check Stats and worn gear, and save before each question if you want to compare them.

A confirmed Macsen relationship leads into his evening scene. Killigan can reconsider that morning's answer about sex in the evening. `I'll top` and `I'll bottom` give different Memories; the later shared-bed images do not require sex. Without a confirmed Macsen partner, `Pursue a romantic relationship with Zhokhar?` appears after the beach if Killigan started the Day 3 trial **or** grew close enough through earlier time together. `Yes` here is the formal choice; `Competition` is not required. Agreeing to sex and choosing a position come afterward, separately. Refusing sex still allows the embrace and kiss. To compare the two partners or Zhokhar's `Yes` / `No`, keep saves before the earlier relationship split. See [Relationships](../reference/relationships.md#zhokhar-spiceport) and [each Memory](../collectibles/memories.md#spiceport-memories).

## Day 8: before leaving

This day follows a set story: another General Store visit, the harbor conflict, and combat or pursuit. There are no new free-exploration slots. Ordinary shop stock remains for sale; the Charm-lock back-room topic from Days 5–6 needs an earlier free-time save. Five Day 8 Memories appear in the latter part of the main story. You do not need to collect each with a different combat outcome. Winning, losing, and stat rewards can differ. Later, the story asks you to check Equipment and put on a weapon before continuing. Owning and wearing are different, and a Dressing Room appearance cannot stand in for battle gear.

Before departure, check that you completed the Day 2–4 excursions you wanted, chose a viable Day 5–6 park or shop branch, and made the Day 7 partner decision you intended. The east and west city maps are map-list records added by the story, not a separate inventory **Map** pickup. Macsen's `Can I keep it?` painting exchange is a character interaction, with no new memento pickup notification in this chapter. The next volume is [Blueleaf Grove](blueleaf-grove.md).
