# Killigan’s Treasure Guide

**Killigan’s Treasure** is a furry visual novel by Eddio. This unofficial guide to **Public v0.57a** covers journey choices, relationships and Personality differences, and the conditions for Memories, Codex entries, and equipment.

Coverage runs from the opening through the end of **Blueleaf Grove Day 12**, after the evening camp scene. The guide covers the current public release. Day numbers follow the dates shown in that part of the game.

The guide contains story and branch spoilers. Read the detailed steps after reaching the relevant scene. On a first playthrough, start with your current region; returning players and anyone with save or unlock questions can begin with [Help](help.md).

## About this guide {#kt-site-info}

This guide covers **Public v0.57a**. Specific display issues are explained in the [Dressing Room entries](collectibles/dressing-room.md#dressing-room-locator); see [Help](help.md#help-trailmarkers) for older saves, device changes and unlock records.

Make a separate regular save before relationship decisions, limited free-time choices, or mutually exclusive collectibles. **Trailmarkers** provide chapter starting points; they do not recreate your old items, relationships, or exploration history.
