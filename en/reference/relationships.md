## Three stages of relationship development {#three-things-to-keep-separate}

- **Affection:** Time together and choices can bring characters closer. Closeness alone does not confirm a partner.
- **Romantic intention:** Killigan or another character considers taking things further. A later decision can still change that direction.
- **Formal confirmation:** A clear choice to become partners. Macsen and Zhokhar have different decision points and follow-ups.

## Preparing for relationship events

Use the companionship and activity steps below to prepare for invitations. If the option you need does not appear, reload a regular save early enough to change its prerequisites.

<a id="prepare-macsen"></a>

### Macsen: Aris Days 4, 7, and 8

One reliable early plan is to choose `Sure, why not.` while traveling together on Day 1; pay for the meal and hear his ideas on Day 2 (`Pay it.`, `Sure, let's hear 'em.`); and choose `Stand up for Mac` during the Day 3 argument with Elise. On Day 4, check whether `Model for him?` appears in the evening conversation. Choose `Sure` if you want the modelling scene. This is a useful preparation route, **not a checklist in which every step is required**. The invitation itself is the signal to check. Save before the Day 4 conversation; if it does not appear, return to an earlier companionship choice.

If you continue this route and accept the modelling invitation, watch for `Invite him to my bed` under `Should I act on it?` on Day 7. If the morning left Killigan dirty, cleaning up at the **Bathhouse** costs two afternoon time slots before you invite Macsen. On Day 8, if `Should I ask him?` appears, answer `Sure` to share a bed, then choose `Romantic` at the later feelings question to see the embrace that night. Save before the Day 7 invitation and the Day 8 feelings question. If you miss both nights, the same Memory has later opportunities at the Day 10 massage or Day 11 balcony. Day 8 `Romantic` is **not required** for the formal Day 11 decision.

<a id="prepare-zhokhar"></a>

### Zhokhar: the Day 3 trial and Day 7 decision in Spiceport

To prepare for the private talk after the Day 3 dinner, do **not formally confirm Macsen** on the Aris balcony. A workable route is to choose `Help treat him` on Crystal Plains Day 5, share a room with Zhokhar on Spiceport Day 1, visit the Warehouse on Day 2 or 3, and choose `Sure` at `Get a closer look?`. The private talk after dinner is your signal that the preparation was enough. `Competition` begins a trial you can reconsider and leads to two Day 3 images; `Support` brings you closer but does not start that trial. A Warehouse visit on Day 4 is too late to help with the Day 3 talk. Save before the Day 3 dinner to compare its answers. If no talk appears, return to an earlier preparation or Aris balcony save.

Zhokhar's Day 6 Bareleaf invitation depends on whether you chose the `Competition` trial, **not just on general closeness**. After the Day 7 beach events, `Pursue a romantic relationship with Zhokhar?` is the formal partner choice. The Day 3 trial is one route toward it, not the only one. Choose `Yes` to confirm the relationship; sex and position are separate later choices. If the Day 7 question does not appear, return to a save where you can still change earlier time together or the relationship branch. Save before the `Yes` / `No` decision if you want both outcomes.

<a id="prepare-ray"></a>

### Ray: the invitation to share his nest in `???`

Closeness with Ray is local to this night. It is separate from Macsen's and Zhokhar's ongoing affection and from confirmed relationships. You must not have confirmed Macsen on the Aris balcony. One direct plan is to answer `Yes` to the faith question, make `Bugs on a stick` for `Ray`, then finish either dancing or ring toss. You can still get the nest invitation if that last activity's interaction fails.

<details class="kt-read-more">
<summary>Alternative plan after answering No to faith</summary>

If you answer `No` to the faith question, do all three night activities, make the bugs for Ray, and succeed at least once at dancing or ring toss. Ring toss needs **both** interactions to succeed.

</details>



Once Ray invites you, you can accept or refuse; save before the sleeping choice to compare them. If Macsen is already your partner, return to the Aris balcony. If you did too few activities, return to the faith question or the start of the night activities.

## Marks beside Memories titles

Memories relationship icons are fixed entry categories; they do not change with your current save's partner status. Macsen and Zhokhar icons mark relationship and intimate content involving that character, including trial or confirmed relationships. The single icon is called `Killigan is single` in the game's help, but it does not replace each scene's acquisition conditions. No icon does not mean no romantic relationship. See the [Gallery icon guide](../collectibles/memories.md) for the shared explanation. A local intimate scene or Zhokhar's trial is not a formal relationship. For example, the [Spiceport Day 5 clutchmates park scene](../collectibles/memories.md#spiceport-memories) requires no confirmed Macsen relationship; Zhokhar's trial does not rule it out. [Ray's nest invitation](#question-mark-ray) also needs enough closeness during that section.

## Cabotte: Redroot Wilds Day 8

After the Bijouk fight, Cabotte's invitation at the inn requires **a win and the Belt still equipped at the post-fight check**. If you lose or the Belt is damaged or removed, the story continues without this invitation. Drinking or eating at the inn changes the presentation that night, but does not replace the fight condition. Choose `Sure` when Cabotte invites you to enter the optional scene. The [Redroot Wilds Day 8 guide](../guide/redroot.md#redroot-wilds-day8) covers preparation and both images.

## Macsen

<a id="macsen-early"></a>

### Early Aris: closeness and sharing a bed

`Stand up for Mac` during the Day 3 argument with Elise brings Macsen closer. The main story continues with other answers, though one leaves out the second image in that night's embrace. See [the two Memories entries](../collectibles/memories.md#aris-day3-hug). If `Model for him?` appears on Day 4, you can decide whether to model; if it does not, you need an earlier companionship choice. See [preparation](#prepare-macsen) and the [modelling Memory](../collectibles/memories.md#aris-day4-model).

On Day 7, you can invite Macsen to your bed if the option appears that evening. He refuses if the morning left Killigan dirty. The **Bathhouse** can restore this opportunity that afternoon for two time slots. You may be able to invite him again on Day 8. Sharing a bed that night still needs `Romantic` at the later feelings question to show the same embrace. The formal relationship decision waits until the Day 11 banquet balcony. See [each day's route to that Memory](../collectibles/memories.md#aris-mac-bedhug).

### Day 8: `Romantic` and `Friendly`

Killigan considers his feelings for Macsen on Aris Day 8. `Romantic` records an intention to pursue romance; `Friendly` treats those feelings as friendship for now. He can reconsider later. The formal decision remains at the banquet balcony, where he can still confirm Macsen even after choosing `Friendly`.

<a id="macsen-day10-massage"></a>

### Day 10: Macsen's massage

If you chose `Romantic` on Day 8, you return to the room with Macsen on Day 10 for a clickable massage scene. Each body area can be selected once; finish four areas to continue.

<details class="kt-read-more">
<summary>Massage areas and screen orientation</summary>

Macsen mentions bruising on his left shoulder and right thigh. For a more comfortable response, avoid those spots and his rear; choose the side of his waist, back, uninjured shoulder, and uninjured thigh. These are **his** body parts, so their positions on screen may differ.

</details>

 The result changes his reaction and closeness, but **every completed massage leads to the bed embrace**. The formal relationship is still decided on Day 11. Save before the interaction to compare responses. See [the other opportunities for the same Memory](../collectibles/memories.md#aris-mac-bedhug).

<a id="macsen-balcony"></a>

### The Aris banquet: the balcony decision

**Direct answer: you can confirm Macsen here without Day 8 `Romantic`. `No` does not confirm anyone else; the formal decision cannot be changed later.**

Enter the Day 11 banquet through Purescale Entryway. Make a separate regular save before Macsen's balcony conversation reaches the question where Killigan can no longer change his mind.

- `Yes` formally confirms Macsen as Killigan's partner, then leads into the confession and kiss. You **do not** need to have chosen Day 8 `Romantic` or reached a particular affection value first.
- `No` leaves the relationship unconfirmed and clears the earlier romantic intention. The two balcony confession and kiss Memories will not appear on that branch; the story continues with the rest of the banquet.

This commitment also changes later relationship and optional content. After confirming Macsen, Zhokhar's [private trial and Day 7 formal decision](#zhokhar-spiceport) are unavailable on that regular playthrough. So are [Ray's nest invitation](#question-mark-ray) and the [adult clutchmates invitation in Spiceport on Day 5](../guide/spiceport.md#spiceport-day5). Choosing `No` only keeps those possibilities open: their own closeness, date, and local choice conditions must still be met.

### How this relates to Memories

After balcony `Yes`, the lead-in to the confession and the kiss appear in order as **two separate Macsen Memories** under **Aris (p.2)**. Day 8 `Romantic` does not affect those acquisition steps. Both display `Aris Day 11` with a Macsen relationship icon. Use the [Memories / CG lookup](../collectibles/memories.md) to tell them apart.

The normal Crystal Plains Day 10 Codex update adds **Cute Things He Does** under Macsen if you confirmed him at the balcony; balcony `No` does not give this page. See the [Codex extra-page reference](../collectibles/codex.md#codex-special).

<a id="macsen-shieldfall"></a>

### Shieldfall Vale: an established relationship

If you confirmed Macsen with `Yes` at the Aris balcony, the **third evening talk with him during Shieldfall Vale Days 2–6** offers `Ask him`. Choose it for an intimate scene and a **Shieldfall Vale** Memory with his relationship icon. The third talk can come as early as Day 4 or as late as the **evening of Day 6**. `Hold off` skips the image and uses up that third-talk opportunity. Neither answer reopens the partner decision. See the [Vale evening plan](../guide/crystal-plains-shieldfall.md#shieldfall-vale).

<a id="macsen-spiceport"></a>

### Spiceport: an established relationship

Balcony `Yes` means you automatically share a room with Macsen in Spiceport. His bedside images can appear on Day 5 or 7 mornings; on Day 6 you can join him at Bareleaf Park, and Day 7 leads to his evening relationship scene. This confirmed relationship rules out Zhokhar's Day 3 private trial and the adult clutchmates invitation at the park on Day 5. You can answer the Day 7 morning question about sex with Macsen and reconsider that answer in the evening. `I'll top` and `I'll bottom` lead to different images; skipping sex still leaves the later scene of sharing a bed. See the [Day 7 journey](../guide/spiceport.md#spiceport-day7) and [Memories lookup](../collectibles/memories.md#spiceport-memories).

## Zhokhar: Spiceport Days 3–7

**Direct answer: `Competition` is a trial, not partner confirmation. Only `Yes` at the Day 7 formal question confirms Zhokhar. Enough earlier closeness can also reach that question, so `Competition` is not required. No confirmed Macsen remains a prerequisite. Save before Day 3 dinner and before the Day 7 formal question.**

### Build closeness before the private trial

Your earlier journey choices affect how close you are to Zhokhar on arrival in Spiceport, especially whether you helped treat him on Crystal Plains Day 5. Start preparing earlier if you want his relationship route. Sharing a room on Spiceport Day 1 adds a separate Day 2 morning image and brings you closer. Agreeing to look more closely during the Day 2–4 Warehouse excursion also helps. **To use that excursion for the Day 3 dinner talk, visit the Warehouse on Day 2 or 3.** Day 4 still allows the excursion itself, but comes too late for that talk. Formal confirmation waits until Day 7.

The private talk after the Day 3 dinner needs enough prior closeness and **no confirmed Macsen relationship** from the Aris balcony. The [early preparation](#prepare-zhokhar) gives one workable route. If the talk does not appear, return to a regular save early enough to change time together or the excursion; you cannot add later activities after dinner to trigger this trial. Once the talk begins:

- `Competition` starts a trial of competition and intimacy that can later be reconsidered. It brings you closer and shows two separate Day 3 Zhokhar Memories. It also opens his Day 6 Bareleaf invitation and the **Things to Tease About** page at the earlier Codex update in this chapter.
- `Support` brings you closer and continues the story, but does not start that trial or grant those two Day 3 images. It **does not rule out** the later Day 7 partner question if you have no confirmed Macsen relationship. Check whether that question appears when you reach it.

Save before the Day 3 dinner to compare those answers. Sharing a room helps with closeness, but is not an individually required prerequisite; the entrance also depends on earlier time together and the Macsen relationship state.

### Day 6 invitation and Day 7 decision

The Day 6 Bareleaf invitation checks whether you entered the **Day 3 trial**. A confirmed Macsen relationship brings Macsen's invitation instead; otherwise, Zhokhar invites you if you started his trial. `Sure` enters that character's scene and skips afternoon free exploration; `Nah` leaves time for the store, fishing, or other targets. See the [Day 6 trade-off](../guide/spiceport.md#spiceport-day6).

After the Day 7 beach, `Pursue a romantic relationship with Zhokhar?` appears if Macsen is not a confirmed partner and you have **enough earlier closeness or the Day 3 trial**. `Yes` is the formal partner choice. `Competition` can help you reach this point, but is not required. `No` leaves the relationship unconfirmed and ends an existing trial. Save before this final question. After `Yes`, the embrace and kiss do not require agreeing to sex. `Absolutely` / `Nah` decide whether to have sex; `I'll top` / `I'll bottom` then select different images. You cannot see both positions in one scene. See the [individual images and special prerequisite](../collectibles/memories.md#spiceport-zhokhar-night).

Keep separate saves before the Aris balcony to compare formal relationships with Macsen and Zhokhar. Spiceport's Codex pages update according to relationship state; check a character's base entry separately from a conditional page. The [Codex lookup](../collectibles/codex.md#codex-special) lists their titles and update points.

<a id="blueleaf-zhokhar"></a>

### Blueleaf Grove: one later Zhokhar image

After the Day 11 spar with Zhokhar, choose `Undress the dragon`. If you formally confirmed the relationship with `Yes` on Spiceport Day 7, a Blueleaf Grove Memory with his relationship icon appears. Having sex with him in Spiceport changes the presentation here, **not whether you get the entry**; the spar result does not gate it either. If you never confirmed Zhokhar, reload before the [Spiceport Day 7 decision](#zhokhar-spiceport). If you confirmed Macsen even earlier, branch from before the [Aris balcony](#macsen-balcony). If Zhokhar is already confirmed and you only chose `Find the round boys` in Grove, a save before the two choices after the Day 11 spar is enough. The [Blueleaf Grove journey](../guide/blueleaf-grove.md#blueleaf-day11) and [Memories lookup](../collectibles/memories.md#blueleaf-memories) cover the local save point and entry. The three evening-camp gifts depend on consecutive talks, not on dating their giver.
