# Personality and local differences

For Killigan’s Treasure Public v0.57a. Choices along the journey shift Personality. The displayed Noble, Neutral, or Barbaric tendency can affect lines, actions, and a few choices. Use the [journey overview](../index.md) to find the local event.

## What are Noble, Neutral, and Barbaric?

The Personality icon and pointer in **Stats** show whether Killigan currently leans **Noble, Neutral, or Barbaric**. These are broad tendencies: two saves with the same label can still offer different dialogue or responses in a local scene. Direction icons beside choices show which way the tendency moves, and later choices can change it further. The entries below distinguish differences caused by Personality from choices that shift it.

## Does Neutral have its own content?

**Yes, including distinct local actions.** The [Zhokhar-room morning on Spiceport Day 2](#spiceport-day2-personality) has three noticeably different responses, with an action of its own for the middle tendency. Keep regular saves early enough to reach different tendencies if you want to compare them.

## Differences by region

### Redroot Wilds Day 1: choosing a tendency

`Someone noble.` and `Someone barbaric.` move Killigan in the corresponding directions; `I'm not sure...` does not make that particular move. The spirit's opening `Truth` / `Ideals` and `Betray` / `Uphold` answers are a separate set of value choices. For Cabotte's Day 8 scene, follow the [fight and Belt conditions](../guide/redroot.md#redroot-wilds-day8); no particular Personality tier is required.

<a id="aris-bathhouse-personality"></a>

### Aris Days 3 / 5 / 6 / 7: Bathhouse clothing

On these four Bathhouse visits, Personality changes how much lower-body clothing Killigan removes on arrival:

- More **Noble-leaning**: keeps lower-body outer clothing and underwear on.
- **Middle tendency**: removes lower-body outer clothing but keeps underwear.
- More **Barbaric-leaning**: removes underwear as well.

The presentation depends on what he is actually wearing when he enters. These are arrival-clothing differences; the bath continues through the same content afterward.

<a id="aris-day4-personality"></a>

### Aris Day 4: what to wear while modelling

Use [Macsen's early preparation](relationships.md#prepare-macsen) to reach the `Model for him?` invitation on Day 4. Choose `Sure` when it appears to enter the scene. A langou or Summit Attire is available as clothing; `Nothing` opens when Killigan is **more Barbaric-leaning**. It may be visible while unavailable, so choose it only when **currently selectable**. All three outfits lead to the **same Memory**, with different presentation. Save before the invitation to compare outfits, or use an earlier save if you need a different Personality tendency. See [acquisition and replay](../collectibles/memories.md#aris-day4-model).

<a id="aris-komoli-personality"></a>

### Aris Day 6: answering Komoli

A sufficiently **Noble-leaning** Killigan can choose the Noble-badged `Defend myself` response and explain why he is inside the Academy. The two explanations lead to different reactions during the local questioning. A separate aggressive response also reads `Defend myself`; use the icons beside the options to distinguish them. Save before meeting Komoli if you want to compare these responses.

### Aris Day 7 morning: the clothing question

Before leaving his Tavern room, a more **Noble-leaning** Killigan dresses automatically, while a more **Barbaric-leaning** Killigan leaves undressed. The middle tendency receives `Put something on?`:

- `It'd be rude not to`: dresses and moves toward **Noble**.
- `Who cares`: leaves undressed and moves toward **Barbaric**.

This clothing choice does not gate the later `Sure` response to the outhouse invitation. To compare the three preparations, keep earlier regular saves with different Personality tendencies.

<a id="aris-purescale-personality"></a>

### Aris Day 7: a brief Purescale Entryway visit

A sufficiently **Noble-leaning** Killigan can select `Question their reasoning`, then claim an escort. `The Beastslayer` successfully bluffs the guard and allows a brief early Purescale visit. Claiming `Headwizard Komoli` fails. `Matagoffe` may also appear after the relevant armor-help event, but that claim fails too.

**Every attempt uses one free-time slot, including choosing to leave; the entryway then closes for the day.** A successful bluff gives a brief visit, with no lasting permit or unique collectible awarded on leaving. Save before spending the slot if you want to compare the guard's responses.

<a id="aris-day7-personality"></a>

### Aris Day 7: dialogue in the outhouse

Personality also changes one of Killigan's thoughts inside the Tavern's outhouse. It does not block the later `Sure` response to the stranger's invitation. For the adult scene, follow the [Day 7 choice reminder](../guide/aris.md#aris-day7-tavern). The [Day 7 Memories entry](../collectibles/memories.md#aris-day7-outhouse) identifies the image and covers SFW settings and saves.

To compare accepting and refusing the invitation, save before the choice in the outhouse. To compare Personality-driven opening actions or dialogue, you need an earlier save that lets you follow different Personality choices.

<a id="aris-day8-personality"></a>

### Aris Day 8: Macsen's morning visit

A more **Barbaric-leaning** Killigan responds casually while exposed; other tendencies cover up and apologize. Earlier lodging and bed-sharing choices determine which version of the morning appears. Personality changes the immediate action and reply, and the later morning schedule is shared.

<a id="aris-banquet-personality"></a>

### Aris Day 11: the banquet's 2000 Ouros

`Take it` accepts the **2000 Ouros**. Two refusals open according to the current tendency. They may be visible while unavailable, so choose them only when **currently selectable**:

- More **Barbaric-leaning**: `Cast it aside`, swatting away the money before asking for help for Aris's beasts.
- More **Noble-leaning**: `Give it to the beasts of Aris`, politely asking for the money to be shared among Aris's beasts.

Both refusals leave you without the money; the difference is in the response. A save before the money question lets you compare answers already available at that point; use an earlier save to change Personality. See the [banquet journey](../guide/aris.md).

<a id="crystal-stargazing-personality"></a>

### Crystal Plains: stargazing with Macsen

With Macsen formally confirmed as your partner, the third romantic camp talk with him in Crystal Plains leads to stargazing. More **Noble-leaning** keeps lower-body clothing on; the middle tendency removes outer lower-body clothing; more **Barbaric-leaning** also removes underwear, depending on what is worn. All three versions continue to the stargazing scene. This is the Crystal Plains camp talk; see [Macsen's later events](relationships.md#macsen-shieldfall) for the adult image from Shieldfall Vale's third talk.

<a id="crystal-day5-personality"></a>

### Crystal Plains Day 5: helping Zhokhar

After finding Zhokhar, `Help treat him` moves Personality toward **Noble** and brings you closer to him; `Leave him be` moves it toward **Barbaric**. The dialogue before the treatment choice varies with the current tendency, and both answers remain available. Macsen continues treating Zhokhar either way. Save before the question if you want both versions; you do not need to change your displayed tier for the single Day 5 Memory. See the [Crystal Plains journey](../guide/crystal-plains-shieldfall.md#crystal-plains).

<a id="spiceport-day2-personality"></a>

### Spiceport Day 2: Zhokhar's room and Neutral's distinct action

After staying in Zhokhar's room on Day 1, an incident the next morning has three visible responses:

- More **Noble-leaning**: interrupts Zhokhar and directs him to the hall toilet.
- **Middle tendency**: grabs a bucket to catch the spill.
- More **Barbaric-leaning**: lets the incident finish, then cleans up afterward.

The middle tendency has its own way of handling it. All three versions return to the same morning sequence.

<a id="spiceport-personality"></a>

### Spiceport: park choices and Personality

These choices shift Personality. You can help at the Residential Streets nursery once on Day 3 or 4. It reopens on Day 5, and can still be available during Day 6 free exploration if you did not do it on Day 5. Each time, `Help out` moves Personality toward Noble and `Leave` toward Barbaric, but **both choices end with Killigan helping**. The visible payment can be 50 or 25 Ouros, depending on Wits with currently worn equipment, not on the Personality icon.

Choosing whether to undress while visiting Bareleaf Park alone also shifts tendency. The adult scenes on a return visit depend on `Rub one out` or `Pour one out`, **not on reaching a Noble, Neutral, or Barbaric tier**. In the Day 4 or 6 solo park scene, walking undressed gives a visible **Charm +5**; staying clothed gives **Wits +5**. That local stat difference does not lock either image behind a Personality tier. See the [park schedule and Day 6 recovery](../guide/spiceport.md#spiceport-day6).

<a id="spiceport-day5-personality"></a>

### Spiceport Day 5: returning to the Library discussion

While talking to Komoli, more **Noble-leaning** politely asks her to return to the subject, the middle tendency asks directly, and more **Barbaric-leaning** interrupts much more aggressively. All three responses lead back to the same discussion topics.

<a id="spiceport-day7-personality"></a>

### Spiceport Day 7: the choice to stay naked

If Killigan is already sufficiently covered, preparation proceeds normally. Otherwise, a sufficiently **Barbaric-leaning** Killigan can continue undressed; other tendencies return to cover up first. The same beach sequence follows.

<a id="blueleaf-personality"></a>

### Blueleaf Grove Day 10: answers while traveling

These travel choices shift Personality, with wording that first depends on whether Killigan is wearing **Undies** or a **Belt**. If he is, `"It feels better"` moves toward **Noble**; `"True...but this feels better"` moves toward **Barbaric** and removes the clothing. If he is not, `"You're right..."` moves toward **Noble** and puts on a **Marchanide Langou**; `"You're damn right."` moves toward **Barbaric**. Both states then reach `Cool things down` / `Join in`: the first moves further toward **Noble**, while the second opens the optional Codex character pages **Balls Research (by Mac)** in that day's update. This choice does not decide the three camp gifts. Save before the relevant answer if you want to compare Codex, Personality, and clothing. See the [Day 10 journey](../guide/blueleaf-grove.md#blueleaf-day10).
