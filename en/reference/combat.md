# Combat & QTEs

For Killigan’s Treasure Public v0.57a. Days follow the dates shown in each region.

<a id="combat-result"></a>

## Combat quick reference

**Complete the QTE, then read the opponent’s dodge or block feedback before choosing your next action.** Find fights below by region and Day.

<a id="combat-equipment"></a>

### Before a fight

Check worn gear in **Equipment** and attributes in **Stats**. Make a regular save before comparing branches. After [camp repairs](../collectibles/equipment.md#practical-items), check that gear is equipped.

<a id="combat-qte"></a>

### QTE input reference

In **QTEs** settings, choose `No Rapids` for easier rapid tapping or `None` for automatic standard QTEs. [Device controls and settings](../help.md#help-qte).

<details class="kt-read-more">
<summary>Show the five common QTE inputs</summary>

::: {.kt-adaptive-records}

| Prompt / type | What to do |
|---|---|
| `Tap rapidly!` | Repeatedly click the on-screen button or press Space. `No Rapids` changes this to `Hold till Fully Charged!`; hold instead. |
| `Hold to Charge up!` | Hold the button, then release in the target area. |
| Timing bar | Watch the moving bar and click the button in the target area. |
| `Wait for the "!"` | Wait for the exclamation mark before pressing the button. |
| `Hold and keep it steady!!` | Hold and release to adjust the bar, keeping it in the target area until the interaction ends. |

:::

</details>

## Redroot Wilds

After leaving Redroot, follow the encounters on Days 2, 5 and 8.

<a id="combat-wilds-early"></a>

### Day 2 → Day 5: creatures on the road

In both fights, choose `Attack it`, complete the charge input, then handle the enemy-turn reaction prompt. Punches weaken the creature; an escape opportunity at low health also lets the journey continue. **Winning, escaping or losing all return to the story**, with extra Ouros for a victory. Check and repair damaged gear at night; reload a pre-fight save to compare outcomes. [Wilds journey](../guide/redroot.md#redroot-wilds).

<a id="combat-bijouk"></a>

### Day 8: Bijouk and Cabotte’s invitation

**Make a regular save before the cry for help and equip `Belt`.** Cabotte’s invitation that night requires beating Bijouk and still wearing Belt afterwards. Follow the current actions and QTE prompts during combat, then check Belt’s worn state.

Losing, or winning after Belt has come off, gives and equips Marchanide Langou before the story continues. Winning with Belt still worn can lead to the inn invitation; choose `Sure` to enter the scene. Reload the pre-cry save to compare equipment and invitation outcomes, and save again after receiving the invitation to compare scene branches. [Local conditions and the two scenes](../guide/redroot.md#redroot-wilds-day8).

## Aris

The Day 2–3 story encounters come first, followed by Coliseum training, the Day 9 Summit and the Day 10 event.

<a id="combat-aris-day2"></a>

### Day 2: first encounter with Zhokhar

Choose an action when `Attack him`, `Talk to him` and `Defend` appear to continue the confrontation. Follow the story choices after Macsen intervenes, then escape back to the academy. Later relationship decisions are covered under [Relationships](relationships.md#zhokhar-spiceport). Save before the encounter to compare these responses.

<a id="combat-elise"></a>

### Day 3: three rounds with Elise

This spar introduces round scoring: score when you hit and your opponent does not; both hitting or both blocking gives a tie. Choose `Punch to the gut` for a charge, `Elbow in the side` for a timed click, or `Shoulder against her` for a reaction input. All three can land when performed successfully; complete the strength-based defense afterwards.

Check the stat results and Ouros Elise gives you, then continue the local story. Reload a regular save from before the spar to compare actions, scores or rewards. [Early Aris journey](../guide/aris.md).

<a id="combat-goal"></a>

### Days 5–8: planning arena training

**Each Coliseum entry costs two slots and allows at most two spars.** Rematch or change opponents after the first bout; leave after the second. For Prime Attire, finish one spar with each of Matagoffe, Tofi and Atama, regardless of the result. Allow at least two visits. See the [four prerequisites](../collectibles/equipment.md#prime-attire) for the other preparations and the first-entry reward window on Days 7–8.

Wagering is optional: a win earns the stake, a loss costs it, and a tie changes nothing. Higher wagers also make the spar harder. For practice or completing an opponent requirement, start without a wager and keep money for supplies. Check the results afterwards and switch to a missing opponent after the first bout. Reload a pre-entry save to compare strategies or schedules.

<a id="combat-matagoffe"></a>

### Matagoffe: distract him through conversation

During training, choose `Talk to him` and answer `Handsome` at the first question to distract him. Then choose `Punch to his gut` and complete the charge: a successful punch can connect. Defend in each round. Check the score and stats afterwards; finishing the spar completes the Matagoffe requirement for Prime Attire. Reload a save from before this bout to compare conversation responses.

<a id="combat-tofi"></a>

### Tofi: keep landing punches, then defend

During training, choose `Punch to his gut` and complete the QTE. He may parry the first successful punch. After that narration, keep using it: later successful punches can hit. Defend after attacking in every round; landing a hit and blocking his attack wins the round.

Check the score and stats afterwards. Finishing the spar completes the Tofi requirement for Prime Attire, leaving you free to plan other activities. Reload a pre-bout save to practise or compare outcomes. [Aris journey](../guide/aris.md#days-58-free-exploration).

<a id="combat-atama"></a>

### Atama: disrupt his vision, then punch

During training, choose `Outsmart him` first. Once the kicked-up dirt disrupts his vision, switch to `Punch to his gut` and complete the charge; a successful punch can connect. Defend afterwards in each round. Check the score and stats at the end; finishing the bout completes the Atama requirement for Prime Attire. Reload a pre-bout save to compare actions.

<a id="combat-summit"></a>

### Day 9 Summit: qualifiers → champion rounds → Zhokhar

**Check that Summit Attire or Prime Attire is worn and make a regular save before competing.** The physical attire worn in the competition is removed afterwards; see [Equipment](../collectibles/equipment.md#prime-attire) for mementos and appearances.

The qualifiers begin with three spars, followed by the log toss and jump. Spar actions are `Punch`, `Dodge` and `Counter`: punch and then defend, or dodge/block first and complete the charge counterattack. Scores affect ranking and dialogue before the story reaches the champion challenge. A failed log toss offers one retry.

The champion rounds progress through **Tofi → Atama → Matagoffe**. A loss ends that opponent sequence early and moves on to Zhokhar’s challenge. The actions become `Gutpunch`, `Risky Strike` and `Timed Bash`, with different approaches for each opponent:

- **Tofi:** use `Timed Bash` and complete the reaction input; once his rhythm is disrupted, use `Gutpunch` and complete the charge. Defend in each round.
- **Atama:** use `Risky Strike` and complete the timed click to break his guard, then switch to `Gutpunch`. Handle his return attack afterwards.
- **Matagoffe:** the first successful `Gutpunch` can hit, after which he adapts to it. Focus on each round’s timed dodge and follow the score through the match.

Zhokhar’s challenge and the subsequent story encounter continue the main plot. Follow the current actions and QTE prompts, then check the related Memories. Reload a save before the relevant match to compare scores or performances. [Summit and Day 10](../guide/aris.md#day-9-athlima-summit-and-its-aftermath).

<a id="combat-aris-day10"></a>

### Day 10: hold the line and follow Binini’s plan

In the creature encounter after graduation, choose `Dodge` for a timed dodge or `Defend` to control the defense bar while holding off its attacks. When Binini proposes his plan, follow the reaction, charge and timing prompts to continue the story. Check the stat results and gear afterwards. The images appear with the main plot; save before fighting to compare input outcomes. [Regional guide](../guide/aris.md#day-9-athlima-summit-and-its-aftermath).

## Crystal Plains

Ordinary Echo fights come first, followed later by the story encounters with Sir Dion and a Bovolian.

<a id="combat-echo"></a>

### Days 2, 4 and 7: Echo battle reference

Days 2 and 4 can feature different Echo types; Day 7 features an Arcanics Echo. The ordinary actions are `Attack`, `Evade` and `Defend`. Read the opponent’s behavior and enemy-turn prompt, then use the matching input.

::: {.kt-adaptive-records}

| Opponent and visible behavior | Approach | Key input |
|---|---|---|
| **Airinetics Echo**: evasive; direct attacks miss | Choose `Evade`; a successful dodge weakens it, while failure hurts you | Click as the bar reaches the target; its return attack also uses a timed dodge |
| **Hydronetics / Solinetics Echo**: bears down with force on its turn | Use `Attack` to strike, then hold your defense against its return attack | Charge to attack; hold/release to control the defense bar |
| **Phytonetics Echo**: suddenly rushes you | Use `Attack` to strike, then react to its return attack | Charge to attack; wait for `!` before pressing |
| **Pyronetics Echo**: moves erratically to draw out a strike | Use `Attack` to strike, then prepare to intercept its return attack | Release in the charge target area |
| **Arcanics Echo**: powerful impact | Use `Attack` to strike, then hold it off | Charge to attack; rapidly tap for its return attack, or hold with `No Rapids` |

:::

**Victories give extra Ouros; companions help after a loss and the journey continues.** Check damaged gear afterwards, repair it at camp and check worn state again. Reload a pre-fight save to practise against the current Echo. [Crystal Plains journey](../guide/crystal-plains-shieldfall.md#crystal-plains).

### Day 9 → Day 10: Sir Dion and the Bovolian

On **Day 10**, face the Bovolian first, then Sir Dion again. Follow the changing action prompts through the shared crisis and aftermath.

The dreadstone Memories appear along the main plot. Save before fighting to compare immediate actions and dialogue, then continue with [the later Crystal Plains sequence and `???`](../guide/crystal-plains-shieldfall.md#question-mark-sequence).

### Day 8: Banni → soldiers → chase

Fight Banni first, then soldiers in the streets, with `Attack`, `Evade` and `Defend`. Follow the attack and defense prompts through the encounters; companions intervene and the escape continues. The chase then uses rapid tapping, timed clicks and reaction inputs.

After the chase and equipment check, confront Sir Dion again. When `Charge`, `Blitz` and `Strike` appear, choose the current actions to reach the subsequent conflict and departure. <a id="combat-blueleaf"></a>

## Blueleaf Grove Day 11

**Make a regular save before the spar.** This three-round fight with Zhokhar temporarily stows your weapon. Choose `Frontal Assault` or `Sucker Punch` to try landing an attack, complete its input, then defend. He dodges a `Weighty Wallop` attack; Wits still governs the final round’s defense.

A win, loss or tie all continue the journey. Killigan winning shows the Memory of Zhokhar falling; Zhokhar winning shows Killigan falling; **a tie shows both**. Reload the pre-spar save to compare images. Handle the later relationship scene through its own local choices. [Full inputs and follow-up choices](../guide/blueleaf-grove.md#blueleaf-day11).
